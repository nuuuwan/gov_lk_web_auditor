Crop Damage Reporting

This service allows farmers to officially report crop damages. By accurately submitting details about the damaged land, crop type, and personal information, officers can quickly conduct field inspections and implement necessary support measures.

Maize Farmer Registration

All farmers cultivating maize in the 2025/26 Maha season must register in the data system. To do so, please fill out the form available through this link and register.

Agrarian NEWS

Information about special programs conducted by the Department of Agrarian Development, programs and events conducted at the Agrarian Service Center level.

Farmer Information

This enables you to check and update your farmer profile and to request subsidy facilities. Furthermore, it enables you to easily monitor and update the recent activities of your farm and be aware of every action taken from crop cultivation to harvesting, ensuring transparency and control.

ASC Information

This facilitates checking of Agrarian Service Center data such as location of the Agrarian Service Center, contact details, progress of cultivation to harvest, officer details and other information.

Marketing Information

This allows you to see the daily wholesale prices, price fluctuations of crops and other information in all Economic Centers of Sri Lanka.

Weather Forecast

This allows you to select the required location of weather forecast and get more information about the weather forecast accordingly.

Information System for Smart Farming

Agrarian.lk is a web-based platform transforming agriculture in Sri Lanka with its Smart Farming Information System. It empowers farmers by offering tools for profile management, subsidy access, and real-time farm monitoring, ensuring transparency and control over farming operations. The platform provides seamless access to Agrarian Service Center data and market intelligence tools, offering insights into wholesale prices and crop fluctuations. Additionally, integrated weather forecasting aids proactive farm management. Agrarian.lk promotes data-driven, collaborative farming practices, enhancing productivity and sustainability in Sri Lankan agriculture.
