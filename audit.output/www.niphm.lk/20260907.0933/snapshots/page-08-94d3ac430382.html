PROJECT MANAGEMENT DIVISION

Project Management Division is a Newly Establish Division at the End of Year 2016. The Main Purpose of the Division is to Planning and Implementing the Development Projects of the Institute. Development Projects are Conducted to Disseminate the Appropriate New and Existing Post-Harvest Technologies of Agricultural Produce to Solve the Problems in Supply and Value Chains and to Uplift the Living Standard of Stakeholders. The Projects are Implemented Mainly With the Collaboration of Extension and Research Divisions.
