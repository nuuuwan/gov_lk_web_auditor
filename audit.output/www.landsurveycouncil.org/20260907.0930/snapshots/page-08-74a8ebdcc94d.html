(Gazette Notification on 2417 dated 2024-12-27)

SCHEME to recognize Academic and Professional qualifications in land surveying, establish practical experience requirements and conduct qualifying examinations and professional interviews, for candidates for registration as registered surveyors and for the issue of annual practicing licence (Section 37 (f) of the Survey Act, No. 17 of 2002).

Registration in the Land Survey Council

(1) A person who has the academic qualifications set out to in paragraph (a) of the Schedule to the Survey Act, No. 17 of 2002 (hereinafter referred to as the “Act”), shall submit an application substantially in Form (i) hereto together with the application fee of Rs. 10,000.00 to the Land Survey Council (hereinafter referred to as the “Council”) for gaining the practical experience referred to in paragraph (b) of that Schedule.

(2) Upon the receipt of the applications referred to in sub section (1), the Council shall request the Surveyor General to conduct an initial practical training for three months. The course fee (of Rs. 15000.00) shall be paid by the applicant prior to the date of commencement of the course.

(3) After completion of the initial practical training, the applicant shall sit for the evaluation test conducted by the Institute of Surveying and Mapping in terms of the criteria determined by the Surveyor General from time to time. The applicant who passes the evaluation test shall be referred to the Surveyor General and who will inform the Land Survey Council to identify the rest of the practical training.

(4) The balance practical training (After initial training) shall be acquired from the Survey Department or from a Registered Licensed Surveyor who is in the panel which has been approved by the Council, based on their experience, technical / practical capabilities and satisfactory professional conduct.

(5) According to the preference of trainees who pass the initial practical training, Council shall inform Surveyor General as two separate lists who are seeking the rest of the practical training under the Surveyor General and under a Registered Licensed Surveyor who is in the approved panel.

(6) The Surveyor General will issue a certificate to the applicants, who have completed the requisite practical qualification satisfactorily in accordance with the existing monthly norm criteria laid down by the Surveyor General in respect of land surveying with completion of the duration of 21 months or 33 months as the case may be. General procedure used by the Survey Department for calculating the progress during the period of acquiring practical qualification will be adopted.

(7) For the registration, any applicant who trained under registered licensed surveyor as in subsection (4) above shall be trained for six (6) months, in the Survey Department including the period described in subsection (5), as partial fulfillment of practical experience which is required for registration. The Surveyor General will issue a certificate to applicants, who have completed the requisite practical qualification under Survey Department for six (6) months and under Registered Licensed Surveyor for a continuous period of 15 months or 27 months satisfactorily as the case may be.

(8) The Surveyor General shall issue a certificate of practical experience to an applicant who has completed the relevant practical experience and passed the final evaluation under each channel based on the periodical evaluation directed by him.

(9) A person who has been recruited to the Survey Department as an apprentice shall not be required to follow this initial practical training or sit for the evaluation to obtain the certificate of practical experience. The Surveyor General may issue a certificate of practical experience for the person who trained as an apprentice under him after the period of two, three years of training as an apprentice or ten(10) or fifteen(15) years as the case may be.

(10) A person who recruited in the Survey Department in Form (ii) and a person who obtained a certificate of practical experience under sub regulations (5) or (6) in Form (iii) may submit an application to the Council, substantially together with the registration fee of Rupees 10000.00 for the registration as a Surveyor.

Every surveyor registered with the Council is issued a certificate of registration and is known as a “Registered Surveyor”.
