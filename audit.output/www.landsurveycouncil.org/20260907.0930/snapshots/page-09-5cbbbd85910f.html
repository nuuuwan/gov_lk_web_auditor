For Registered Surveyors

Obtaining the Annual Practising licence

(Gazette Notification on 2417 dated 2024-12-27)

(1) Every registered Surveyor, except those who are employees in Survey Department who applies for the annual practicing licence, from Land Survey Council for the first time substantially in Form (iv) together with the fee of Rs. 20,000.00 to sit for practical examination which is conducted by the Surveyor General.

(2) Upon the request of Council with relevant fee, Surveyor General shall conduct the practical examination periodically and issue the results to Council.

(3) Successful applicants shall present for an interview conducted by the Council and the Council shall issue annual practicing licence, upon the payment of Rs. 6,000.00 as the annual licence-fee, to the applicant who has the academic qualifications specified in the Act and pass the practical examination described in subsection (2).

(4) Sub section 2 shall only apply for registered surveyors who are referred in part 1 to gain practical training for registration. Those who are apply for Annual Practising Licence for the first time after retirement of the Government Survey Service shall apply together with Form (vi).

(5) Every Registered Licensed Surveyor shall apply for the annual practising licence for the succeeding year on or before 31st October of the current year.

A Registered Licensed Surveyor who applies for the annual practising licence in Form (iv) shall submit licence fee and -

  • i. Two colour photographs to the size of 4 cm X 3 cm. One photograph should be pasted on a blank A4 white paper and should be certified by a Justice of Peace as follows “I certify that the photograph above is that of Mr./Ms. ……………………. (Full name)”

  • ii. A certified copy of the National Identity Card (both sides) - (It may be certified by the Secretary of the Land Survey Council or any Senior Officer in the Survey Department who is not below the rank of Snr.SS)

  • iii. Certified copies of your professional certificates. (It may be certified by the Secretary of Land Survey Council, a Justice of Peace or any Senior Officer in the Survey Department who is not below the rank of Snr.SS.

  • iv. Licence fee could be paid at any branch of the Bank of Ceylon in favor of “Chairman, Land Survey Council” Account No 0001658239 – Bank of Ceylon, Narahenpita branch and forward the credit slip to LSC office under registered post. Any other method of paying licence fee is not accepted. (You should get your Registration Number printed by the bank so that it will be included in the current account of Land Survey Council.)

    (6) A Registered Licensed Surveyor who applies for the annual practising licence in Form (v) for the succeeding year shall submit licence fee and -

  • (a) a certified copy of the register of issuance of survey plans (PIR) maintained by him as per the format specified by the Council together with application; and

  • b) the certificates of participation of at least two continuous professional development programmes conducted by the Council, Surveyors’ Institute of Sri Lanka or any other institution accredited by the Council, during the period from 15th September of the previous year to 14th September of the current year immediately prior to the year for the renewal applies, as specified by the Council. Every registered licensed surveyor must attend at least one CPD course conducted by the Council.

    • (7) Upon the satisfaction of the documents submitted by an applicant under sub regulation (6), the Council shall issue the annual practicing license.

    (8) The Fee payable for a certified copy of any Document or register maintained by the council the Council shall be paid Rupees Two Hundred (200/=) per sheet.(8)The Fee payable for a certified copy of any Document or register maintained by the council the Council shall be paid Rupees Two Hundred (200/=) per sheet.

    Every registered surveyor issued with an Annual Practising Licence is known as a “Registered Licensed Surveyor” under the section 41 of Survey Act No. 17 of 2002.

    Continuous Professional Development

    Section 54 of the Survey Act No.17 of 2002 requires a licensed surveyor to follow continuous Development, Programmes related to profession of surveying. Continuous Professional Development (CPD) is a vital component of Land Surveying. Being aware of this, LSC has encouraged Registered Licensed Surveyors to follow training courses/workshops to be in par with present developments in new technologies introduced in the field of Land Surveying by Survey professionals.

    Survey Practice Guidelines

    The Hand book of Guidelines to Registered Licensed Surveyors is a collection of reproduced presentations made at CPD Programmes conducted by the Land Survey Council. The Registered Licensed Surveyors could refer to it if ever they needs any guidance. They are not binding on any surveyor but are merely guide lines. The LSC does not seek to intervene in the relationship the professional surveyor has with his clients. Nevertheless the LSC promotes the system of always signing a contract with the client by the surveyor whenever he undertakes a survey job with regard to “terms of engagement and cost agreement”. A copy of Hand Book of Guidelines could be obtained by any Licensed Surveyor from the LSC office free of charge.

    Occupational Safety and Health (OSH)

    The LSC has not formulated any special guidelines or regulations in this regard. But the Registered Licensed Surveyors need to be mindful about Labour Laws and Workmens’ Compensation Ordinance, which are general laws of the country, when they work with their assistants in the field.
