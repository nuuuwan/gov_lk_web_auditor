Welcome to Milco (Pvt) Ltd

Established in 1956 as the National Milk Board under the Ministry of Agriculture, Milco (Pvt) Ltd has a rich history of commitment to enhancing national milk production and supporting the livelihoods of dairy farmers. Our founding objectives included reducing unemployment and combating malnutrition among children, and these goals continue to guide our mission today.

Our farmers benefit

Supporting Our Farmers

We have established a robust network of 95 milk collection centers (MCCs) linked to 1,423 Farmer Managed Societies (FMS), with approximately 14,500 registered dairy farmers supplying us directly. The average price for a liter of fresh milk is Rs. 160/=. Payments are based on the compositional quality of the milk, primarily its fat and solid content. Milk is accepted only after thorough quality checks.
