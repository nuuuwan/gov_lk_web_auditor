Geotechnical & Foundation Engineering

Our Geotechnical and Foundation Engineering expertise underpins all sectors dealt with by CECB from buildings to infrastructure for water, energy, rail, highways, harbours and ports, reclamation and aviation. To the full range of Geotechnical and Foundation needs, we bring interconnected ground engineering skills, which are complemented by our in-house expertise in the field.

  • Site Investigation and Interpretation
  • Deep Excavation
  • Earthworks
  • Foundations
  • Slope Stabilization and Earth Retaining Systems
  • Ground Improvement
  • Dam Engineering
  • Tunnelling (Soft Ground and Hard Ground)
  • Offshore Geotechnics
  • Instrumentation and Monitoring
