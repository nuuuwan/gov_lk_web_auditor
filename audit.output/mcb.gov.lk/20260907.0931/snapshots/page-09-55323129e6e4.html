Mission

Our mission is to eradicate extreme poverty and promote shared prosperity in Sri Lanka while supporting a livable planet. This goal is challenged by numerous interconnected crises. Time is critical. We need to devise a new strategy to drive impactful development.

Vision

We envision a Sri Lanka free from poverty, emerging as a leading green nation on a sustainable planet while supporting the sustainability of green approaches in the South Asian region.
