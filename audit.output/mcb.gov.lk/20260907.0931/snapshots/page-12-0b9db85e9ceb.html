Development Institutions

Why Partner with Us.​

  • Strategic Collaboration: Align your goals with national priorities and global sustainability targets.

  • Resource Mobilization: Leverage funding and technical assistance to enhance project outcomes.

  • Innovation and Impact: Engage in projects that pioneer new approaches and set benchmarks for success.

    • At MCB, we recognize the critical role that development institutions play in addressing global and local challenges. Through our partnerships with these institutions, we drive initiatives that focus on sustainable agriculture, climate resilience, infrastructure development, and social welfare. By pooling our resources and expertise, we create scalable and impactful solutions that not only benefit Sri Lanka but also contribute to regional and global development goals.

    Executing & Implementing Agencies

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Operational Excellence: Collaborate on projects that are delivered with precision and efficiency.

  • Shared Expertise: Combine strengths to tackle complex challenges and deliver sustainable solutions.

  • Community Impact: Be part of initiatives that directly improve livelihoods and promote environmental stewardship.

    • Executing and implementing agencies are essential to the success of MCB’s strategic initiatives. Our collaboration ensures that projects are not only executed efficiently but also meet the highest standards of quality and impact. Together, we address critical issues like water management, rural development, and climate adaptation, bringing tangible benefits to communities across Sri Lanka.

    Government Sector Partners

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Policy Implementation: Influence and execute national policies that address pressing development needs.

  • Sustainable Growth: Contribute to projects that foster long-term economic and social development.

  • Public Service Enhancement: Collaborate on initiatives that improve infrastructure, healthcare, education, and more.

    • MCB’s partnerships with government sector entities are built on a foundation of trust, collaboration, and shared goals. We work together to implement policies and projects that drive national development, enhance public services, and promote sustainable economic growth. These partnerships are instrumental in achieving our mission to improve the quality of life for all Sri Lankans.

    Inter-Government Agencies

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Regional Collaboration: Work on initiatives that promote regional integration and shared prosperity.

  • Resource Sharing: Access the combined resources and expertise of multiple governments.

  • Large-Scale Impact: Participate in projects that have far-reaching effects beyond national borders.

    • MCB engages with inter-governmental agencies to foster international cooperation and address cross-border challenges. These partnerships are crucial for implementing large-scale projects that require a coordinated approach across multiple jurisdictions, such as regional infrastructure development, disaster risk reduction, and environmental conservation.

    Private Sector Partners

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Innovation and Efficiency: Introduce cutting-edge technologies and management practices into development projects.

  • CSR and Sustainability: Align your corporate social responsibility goals with impactful community projects.

  • Economic Opportunities: Access new markets and business opportunities through collaboration on government-endorsed initiatives.

    • Private sector collaborations are key to bringing innovation, efficiency, and investment to MCB’s projects. We partner with businesses that share our vision for sustainable growth, ensuring that our initiatives are not only economically viable but also socially and environmentally responsible. Together, we create value for both the community and the economy.

    Consultants

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Specialized Expertise: Apply your skills and knowledge to high-impact projects in a government-backed environment.

  • Innovative Solutions: Work on projects that push the boundaries of traditional consultancy and explore new methodologies.

  • Career Growth: Enhance your professional portfolio by contributing to nationally significant projects.

    • MCB values the specialized knowledge and insights that consultants bring to our projects. Our consultants work on a diverse range of initiatives, from infrastructure development to digital transformation, helping us stay ahead of industry trends and deliver innovative solutions that meet the needs of our clients and stakeholders.

    Contractors & Suppliers

    Why Partner with Us.​

  • Reliable Collaboration: Engage in long-term partnerships with a government-supported bureau known for its reliability.

  • Local Industry Support: Contribute to the development of local industries through sourcing and procurement.

  • High-Impact Projects: Participate in large-scale development projects that require excellence in execution.

    • Contractors and suppliers are vital to the successful execution of MCB’s projects. We prioritize partnerships that ensure the timely delivery of high-quality materials and services, supporting the growth of local industries and contributing to the overall success of our initiatives.

    • Corporate and private foundations are crucial partners in driving MCB’s mission forward. These collaborations provide the financial and material resources needed to undertake ambitious projects that deliver long-term benefits to communities and the environment.

    Corporate Procurement

    Independent Observers

    Why Partner with Us.​
