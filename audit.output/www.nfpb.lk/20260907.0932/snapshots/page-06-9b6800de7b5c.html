National Food Promotion Board which simply denotes as NFPB has been incorporated by the Sri Lanka Freedom from Hunger Act No. 15 of 1973 and currently engages in the operations to promote traditional healthy foods that are free of poisonous substances through giving priority to the concept of the sustainable development in Agriculture through a local agri-economy.
