Hector Kobbekaduwa Agrarian Research and Training Institute

Vision

Be the leader for generating and disseminating knowledge for sustainable agrarian and rural development.

Mission

To strengthen agrarian and rural sector through conducting research and training activities.

Goals

  • To make policy process more effective through knowledge generation and timely dissemination
  • To keep the public informed by providing independent assessments on national policy issues
  • To strengthen capacity for socio-economic policy analysis through staff and knowledge investments
  • To increase the capacity of rural development stakeholders through training

Objectives

  • Timely dissemination of market information
  • Identification of agrarian policy perspectives
  • Priority setting in agrarian issues
  • Build partnerships with local and global organizations
  • Foster a motivated, competent team of employees

Strategies

  • Strengthen the Research and Training Committee
  • Agree on thematic areas
  • Uniform publication standards
  • Improve proposal literature reviews
  • External reviews for research reports
  • Boost local language research
  • Engage with universities and donors
  • Introduce performance-based evaluations
  • Organize faculty development activities

Activities

  • Market info collection and analysis
  • Policy evaluation and development assessments
  • Feasibility and impact studies
  • Water resources and environmental research
  • Workshops, training, and seminars
  • Development of staff competencies
  • Library and IT enhancement
  • Strategic recruitment and rotation
