Department of Cinnamon Development

Welcome to the Department of Cinnamon Development

In a significant move to boost Sri Lanka’s cinnamon industry, the government outlined an ambitious plan in Budget Proposal No. 16 of 2023. This proposal aimed to establish a dedicated department for the development of cinnamon, elevating it from a minor export crop to a major commercial plantation crop.

Read More Read More

Vision & Mission

Vision

To position Ceylon cinnamon as the foremost export-oriented plantation crop in Sri Lanka

Mission

Our mission is to strengthen the contribution of the Ceylon cinnamon industry to the national economy and improve the well-being of all stakeholders through strategic initiatives that promote sustainability, elevate quality standards, and broaden market access.

Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation

Department of Cinnamon Development is functioning under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation.
