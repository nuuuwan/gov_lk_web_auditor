About Us

The Department of Animal Production and Health (DAPH) is the state organization responsible for providing technical leadership and support services for the development of livestock Industry in Sri Lanka. The DAPH is located in Peradeniya, in the Hill Capital of Kandy, Sri Lanka is being currently functioned under the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Land and Irrigation.
